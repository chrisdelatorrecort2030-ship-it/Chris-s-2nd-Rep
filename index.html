What Tech or People have Tried Effortly To Stop The Extreme Heat?

  • The MIT climate project has started to develop a proof-of-concept prototype of a wearable personal cooling system
  • Apps are being made from multiple AI companies to reveal heat risk and offer solutions
  • Volunteers around the neighborhoods have been giving out water, cooling towels and fans and have set up shelters
  • And city leaders in Miami and Phoenix have opened emergency cooling centers and direct public health outreach during severe heat waves
